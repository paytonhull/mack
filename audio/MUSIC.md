## Please note

- **Keep the file metadata.** The MP3 files carry a "made with suno" tag (creation date and ID) that Suno adds to its output. Suno's Terms ask that this provenance information is not removed or altered. Copying or re-encoding the files with a tool that preserves tags (for example `ffmpeg -map_metadata 0`) keeps it.
- **No AI training.** Suno's Terms prohibit using its output to train other AI or machine-learning models. Please don't use these recordings for that.
- **Swapping the music.** If you fork M.A.C.K. you can replace the tracks. The app loads `audio/final-count.mp3` and `audio/lingering-dissonance.mp3` by name, so replacing those two files is the only change needed.
