M.A.C.K. (Monitor And Control Kernel)

Your Mac's CPU, GPU, memory, network and disk as a 1980s war room.

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac. The only network use is the optional Live Feed, which plays a YouTube video with YouTube's embedded player. That is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.
