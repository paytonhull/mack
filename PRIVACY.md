# Privacy Policy for M.A.C.K.

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

M.A.C.K. (Monitor And Control Kernel) is a Mac system monitor. **It does not collect, store off your Mac, share or sell any personal data.**

## What the app reads
To draw its screens, M.A.C.K. reads basic statistics from your own Mac: CPU, GPU, memory, network and disk activity, load, uptime, your Mac's name and model, and the names of your busiest running processes. This information is shown on screen and **never leaves your Mac**.

## What the app saves
About an hour of CPU, GPU and memory history is saved inside the app's own storage on your Mac so the charts survive a restart. A few preferences (such as whether sound or music is on) are saved in the app's own storage too. You can delete all of it by deleting the app. M.A.C.K. has no accounts and no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

## The Live Feed (optional)
One screen, **Live Feed**, plays a YouTube video using YouTube's embedded player. It only connects to the internet if you open that screen. When it does:
- the video is loaded from YouTube, so YouTube and Google can receive technical information such as your IP address, as they do whenever a YouTube video is played. This is covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms);
- for a channel (such as NASA's) the app asks YouTube which video that channel is streaming right now. Nothing about you is included in that request.

M.A.C.K. adds nothing to these requests and does not read your YouTube account or history.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
